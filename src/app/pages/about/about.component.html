About Our Free QR Code Generator

What Is This Tool?

Our QR Code Generator is a free, browser-based tool for creating QR codes from any text-based content. It runs entirely in your browser — no data is sent to any server. Your input stays private on your device.

Whether you need a QR code for a website link, a business card, a product label, or a restaurant menu, our generator creates downloadable PNG images in seconds.

How Does It Work?

Built with Angular and powered by the QR code encoding standard (ISO/IEC 18004), our tool converts your text input into a matrix barcode that smartphone cameras can read instantly.

  1. Enter your text, URL, or data into the input field.
  2. Click Generate to create the QR code.
  3. Download the QR code as a PNG image or copy it as a Base64 string for web embedding.

Features

  • 100% free — no cost, no watermarks, no limits
  • Runs entirely in your browser — no account or sign-up needed
  • Instant QR code generation from any text or URL
  • Download as PNG for print-quality images
  • Copy as Base64 for embedding in HTML or emails
  • Dark mode support for comfortable use at any time
  • Mobile-friendly responsive design

Privacy

Your data never leaves your device. All QR code generation happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. We do not collect, store, or transmit your input data.
