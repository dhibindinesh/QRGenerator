What is a QR Code?

A QR Code (Quick Response Code) is a two-dimensional barcode invented in 1994 that stores information in a machine-readable format. It has become the universal standard for instantly sharing data between the physical and digital world.

How does a QR code work? A QR code encodes data as a grid of black and white squares. Error correction allows QR codes to be read even when partially damaged. Smartphone cameras decode the pattern in milliseconds — no dedicated app needed on modern phones.

Can I create QR codes for free? Yes. Our QR code generator is completely free. You can generate unlimited QR codes directly in your browser — no registration, no watermarks, and no limits on downloads.

Can I download QR codes? Yes. Every generated QR code can be downloaded as a high-resolution PNG image or copied as a Base64 string for embedding directly in HTML or emails.
